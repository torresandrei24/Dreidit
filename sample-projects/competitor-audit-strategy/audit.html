📏 Methodology

The 8-Point Audit Framework

Every competitor is scored on the same checklist — this keeps the comparison fair and the report defensible.

  1. Audience size — followers per platform and rough growth trend
  2. Posting cadence — posts per week, consistency, best days & times
  3. Engagement rate — (likes + comments) ÷ followers — health, not vanity
  4. Content mix — Reels vs carousels vs photos vs Stories; pillars used
  5. Brand voice — consistent tone, personality, Pinoy culture touch
  6. Hashtag strategy — count, broad/niche/branded mix, searchable captions
  7. Community management — replies, DMs, UGC reposts, giveaways
  8. Local discovery — geotags, Google Maps, location tags, "near me" cues
🧮 ER FormulaER% = (likes + comments) ÷ followers × 100
🗓️ Snapshot windowLast 30 days of public posts per account
🎯 OutputBenchmark table → gap list → prioritized 90-day actions

Competitor Findings

Local Giant

Coffee Project PH

Founded in Balibago, Angeles City — the client's own city
@coffeeproject.ph
  • Audience: ~1M+ followers
  • Cadence: 3–4 posts/week, reel-heavy
  • ER: ~2–3% on Reels
  • Voice: aesthetic, aspirational
  • Community: slow replies, thin personality
Benchmark

Starbucks PH

The ceiling for campaigns, polish, and reach
@starbucksph
  • Audience: ~3.5M+ followers
  • Cadence: 5–7 posts/week + daily Stories
  • ER: ~1–1.5% (huge reach)
  • Voice: premium, warm, campaign-driven
  • Community: fast but templated
🧩 What nobody does well

The Gap in This Market

  1. Fast, personal replies to every comment and DM
  2. Authentic people content: baristas, regulars, behind-the-scenes
  3. Kapampangan touches and real local community ties
  4. Product education — why the price is fair, how beans are brewed
  5. Clear calls-to-action on every post: visit, order, DM
💡 The giants win on budget and reach — but everyone is weak on community, personality and local heart. That's the space a small shop can win for free.

💡 Gap → Action

Five Opportunities

Opportunity 1
Reply like a human
Block 30 min/day for replies. Target: 100% of comments answered within 2 hours.
Community SOP established
Opportunity 2
People-first content
Weekly barista spotlight + customer-of-the-week Reel. Faces build trust faster than flat lays.
P2 pillar created
Opportunity 3
Local voice
Speak Kapampangan sometimes, tag Angeles City, support barangay and school events.
Own the neighborhood
Opportunity 4
Product education
"Why your coffee costs P30 more" series — builds trust and kills price comparisons.
Trust = defense
Opportunity 5
CTA discipline
Every post ends with exactly one action: Visit, DM, Order ahead, or Tag a friend.
Conversion habit
