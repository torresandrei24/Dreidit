## 2. Audit Framework

Every competitor was scored on the same 8-point checklist. This keeps the
comparison fair and the report defensible.

| # | Dimension | What I look at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audience size | Followers per platform, growth trend (rough) |
| 2 | Posting cadence | Posts per week, consistency, best days/times |
| 3 | Engagement rate | (likes + comments) / followers — the true health metric, not follower count |
| 4 | Content mix | Reels vs carousels vs photos vs Stories; pillars represented |
| 5 | Brand voice | Consistent tone? Personality? Philippine-culture touch? |
| 6 | Hashtag strategy | Count, mix of broad/niche/branded, searchable captions |
| 7 | Community management | Replies to comments, DMs, UGC reposts, giveaways |
| 8 | Local discovery | Geotags, Google Maps link, TikTok/IG location tags, "near me" cues |

**Engagement rate formula used:** `ER% = (likes + comments) ÷ followers × 100`

## 3. Competitor Findings (Estimates — verify before use)

### 3.1 Coffee Project PH (@coffeeproject.ph)

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| Audience | ~1M+ followers across platforms |
| Cadence | 3–4 posts/week, consistent; heavier on Reels |
| Engagement | Healthy for its size (~2–3% on Reels, lower on photos) |
| Content mix | 70% visual-first: drink shots, store interiors, product launches; occasional promos |
| Brand voice | Aesthetic, aspirational, "this is the place to be seen" |
| Hashtags | ~10–20 per post, mix of branded + Filipino coffee + generic |
| Community | Occasional UGC reposts; replies exist but not fast or personal |
| Local discovery | Strong — every store has geotagged posts and check-in gravity |

**What they do well:** visual consistency, real brand recognition, every store
becomes free advertising via check-ins.
**Where they're weak:** community replies are slow, personality is thin,
stories/humor rarely used, and they lean on looks over "people".

### 3.2 Starbucks Philippines (@starbucksph)

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| Audience | ~3.5M+ followers |
| Cadence | 5–7 posts/week + daily Stories; event-driven (seasonal drops) |
| Engagement | Lower rate than Coffee Project (~1–1.5%) but massive reach |
| Content mix | Campaign-driven: seasonal menus, collabs, merch; polished Reels |
| Brand voice | Consistent, premium, warm; strong holiday/mahogany vibes |
| Hashtags | Few but precise; campaigns get their own hashtags |
| Community | Structured replies, quick response; uses Instagram collab features |
| Local discovery | App-driven; store locator; some geotags |

**What they do well:** campaign discipline, Story cadence, launch hype
(sirens, countdowns, seasonal drops).
**Where they're weak:** unreachable for a local shop to match on budget —
but also slower to feel "human" and Filipino.

### 3.3 The gap (what nobody in this market does well)

1. Fast, personal replies to every comment and DM.
2. Authentic, low-polish "people content": baristas, regulars, behind-the-scenes.
3. Kapampangan/local-language touches and local community ties.
4. Genuine product education (brew methods, beans, why the price is fair).
5. Clear calls-to-action (visit, order, DM).

## 4. Benchmark Snapshot

| Metric | Coffee Project | Starbucks PH | Araw Kape (today) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IG followers | ~1M+ | ~3.5M+ | ~1,600 |
| Posts / week | 3–4 | 5–7 | 2 |
| Est. ER on Reels | ~2–3% | ~1–1.5% | ~1% |
| Replies to comments | Slow | Fast, templated | Inconsistent |
| UGC reposting | Rare | Yes | No |
| Local community content | No | No | *Empty space* |

**The insight:** the giants win on budget and reach, but *everyone* is weak on
community, personality, and local heart — the exact things a small local shop
can win on for free.

---

## 5. Five Opportunities (Gap → Action)

| # | Gap found | Action for Araw Kape |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Slow, impersonal replies | 30 min/day blocked for replying; reply to 100% of comments within 2 hours |
| O2 | No human/people content | Weekly "barista spotlight" + customer-of-the-week Reel |
| O3 | No local voice | Speak Kapampangan sometimes, tag Angeles City, support local school events |
| O4 | No product education | "Why your coffee costs 30 pesos more" series — builds trust, fights price comparisons |
| O5 | No CTA discipline | Every post ends with one action: Visit / DM / Order ahead / Tag a friend |

## 6. The 90-Day Strategy

### 6.1 Content pillars (kept simple — 3, not 7)

| Pillar | Share | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **P1: The Product** | 40% | Drinks, beans, brew process — make mouths water |
| **P2: The People** | 35% | Baristas, regulars, neighborhood stories — build community |
| **P3: The Place** | 25% | Store vibes, local events, geotagged visits — own the location |

### 6.2 Cadence (realistic for a solo/small-team social manager)

| Platform | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 3/week (Tue, Thu, Sat) |
| Instagram Stories | Daily, 3–5 frames |
| Facebook | Crosspost Reels + 1 photo/week |
| TikTok | 3/week (same content, re-cut per platform) |

### 6.3 First 2 weeks sample calendar (Week 1)

| Day | Pillar | Content | Hook / caption angle | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue | P1 | Reel: "How we pull our signature espresso" | "The 26-second shot that people drive from Clark for" | Visit |
| Thu | P2 | Reel: Barista spotlight #1 | "Meet Aling Nena — 12 years of mornings" | Tag a friend |
| Sat | P3 | Reel: Store corner tour | "POV: your quiet corner in Angeles City" | Geotag + save |
| Sun | P1 | Story poll: new seasonal drink name | "Name our new drink: Saging or Coco?" | Vote in poll |
| Mon | P3 | Photo: coffee + view (geotagged) | "Some desks need better views" | Tag a friend |
| Wed | P2 | Reel: Customer of the week | "Your regulars are your family" | Tag them |
| Fri | P1 | Reel: beans restock b-roll | "Fresh roast Friday" | Order ahead |

### 6.4 Hashtag & search play

- **3 buckets, 8–10 total:** branded (`#ArawKape`), local (`#AngelesCity`,
  `#PampangaCafe`, `#ClarkCafe`), interest (`#CafeHoppingPH`, `#Coffeeph`,
  `#Kapampangan`).
- Say the search phrase in the first 3 seconds of every Reel: "Best coffee in
  Angeles City" is a *search term*, not a caption.
- Put the location tag on EVERY post — this is the #1 free local growth lever.

### 6.5 Community management SOP (the differentiator)

1. Reply to every comment within 2 hours (alert on phone).
2. Every 5th comment gets a question back — start conversations.
3. Repost UGC to Stories every day there is any (thank + tag).
4. 1 DM audit/week: answer all DMs, log questions as content ideas.
5. Local events: support 1 school/city event/month, post about it.

### 6.6 KPIs for 90 days

| Metric | Start | Day 90 target |
|---|---|---|
| IG followers | 1,600 | 3,000+ |
| Reels avg views | ~300 | 2,000+ |
| Engagement rate | ~1% | 3%+ |
| Comments replied | Inconsistent | 100% within 2h |
| Profile visits / week | n/a | 500+ |
| "Found us on social" in-store votes | n/a | Track via sticker |
