Assets to Prepare

  • Event logo pack + brand colors + font file
  • Speaker headshots + talk titles (confirm consent)
  • Venue photos / video walkthrough
  • Ticket link — UTM-tracked: ?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social
  • 20-question interview list for on-site creators
  • Sponsor booth list + talking points
  • Branded hashtag (#TechCon2027) + alt hashtags
  • Emergency kit: backup phones, power banks, gimbal, lav mic
  • Posting schedule drafted — leave 20% buffer for on-the-day content

📡 LIVE + rules

LIVE Plan & On-the-Day Rules

TikTok LIVE
When to go live
Day 1 opening keynote (30 min) · Day 2 lunch Q&A (20 min) · Day 2 closing keynote (30 min) · Day 3 awards (20 min).
Crew: host + camera + moderator
Post-LIVE
Clip the best moment
Cut the best 30 sec into a normal post within 2 hours. LIVE replays underperform raw clips — always follow up.
Pinned comment = ticket link
Music & consent
Stay compliant
Use TikTok's Commercial Music Library only. Ask before filming attendees; keep a "no photo" badge option for speakers.
Protects the brand
  1. Speed beats polish. Raw, authentic clips posted within 2 hours.
  2. CTA on every post. Ticket link pinned in comments; "follow for day 2" during the event.
  3. Reply to every comment in the first hour. Comments = algorithm fuel; save 20 min/day for replies.

Search tip: put the key phrase in the first 2 lines of the caption AND say it in the first 3 seconds — TikTok search picks up both. Never repeat the same 15 generic hashtags.

🤝 Amplify

Community Moves

  1. Duet/Stitch: day before the event, duet the top 3 "what to expect" videos from creators
  2. Creators in the room: invite 10 local tech creators — free ticket for 1 post + 1 LIVE mention
  3. UGC fuel: photo-booth wall with the branded hashtag; post "best of the wall" on day 2
  4. Crisis templates: pre-written calm replies for overcrowding, speaker cancellation, ticket issues — triage, then hand off to ops
