## 2. Content Pillars

Every piece of content falls under one pillar. This keeps the feed structured
and measurable.

| Pillar | Purpose | Example content |
|---|---|---|
| **P1: Hype & FOMO** | Make people regret missing it | Speaker teasers, venue b-roll, "last 50 tickets" |
| **P2: Value & Education** | Show what attendees learn | 60-sec speaker tips, "3 things I learned at TechCon" |
| **P3: People & Community** | Humanize the event | Attendee interviews, team behind the scenes, sponsor booth tours |
| **P4: Live Ops** | Real-time coverage | LIVE streams, day recaps, "day 1 done" wrap videos |
| **P5: Recap & Next** | Convert this crowd to next year | Highlight reel, testimonial quotes, "see you in 2027" |

**Rule of thumb per day:** 60% P1–P2 (before), 70% P3–P4 (during), 80% P5 (after).

## 3. Pre-Event Phase (4–6 weeks before)

### 3.1 Assets to prepare in advance

- [ ] Event logo pack + brand colors + font file
- [ ] Speaker headshots + talk titles (confirm consent)
- [ ] Venue photos/video walkthrough
- [ ] Ticket link (UTM-tracked: `?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social`)
- [ ] 20-question interview list for on-site creators
- [ ] Sponsor booth list + talking points
- [ ] Branded hashtag (e.g., `#TechCon2027`) + alt hashtags
- [ ] Emergency kit: 2 backup phones, power banks, gimbal, lav mic
- [ ] Posting schedule drafted (leave 20% buffer for on-the-day content)

## 4. During the Event (Day 1–3) — Run of Show

### 4.1 Daily rhythm (same skeleton every day)

| Time | Activity | Format | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | "Day 2 setup" b-roll: crew, coffee, badges | TikTok post (scheduled 8 AM) | Creator A |
| 8:00 AM | Doors open: check-in b-roll + welcome | Story/Reel | Creator A |
| 9:00 AM | Keynote: 15-sec clip within 10 min of it ending | TikTok post | Editor |
| 10:30 AM | Speaker interview #1 (2-min cut) | TikTok post | Creator B |
| 12:00 PM | Lunch crowd shot + "best lunch flex" | TikTok post | Creator A |
| 1:30 PM | Sponsor booth tour | TikTok post | Creator B |
| 3:00 PM | Speaker interview #2 | TikTok post | Creator B |
| 4:30 PM | Day recap draft + day's stats screenshot | — | Editor |
| 6:00 PM | "Day 1 done" wrap video + CTA "join us tomorrow" | TikTok post | Editor |
| 7:30 PM | Post-day team debrief (what hit, what missed) | Meeting | Lead |

### 4.2 TikTok LIVE plan

- **When:** Day 1 opening keynote (30 min), Day 2 lunch break Q&A (20 min), Day 2 closing keynote (30 min), Day 3 awards/announcements (20 min).
- **LIVE crew:** 1 host + 1 camera + 1 comment moderator (rotates every 20 min).
- **LIVE checklist:** horizontal-capable phone, tripod, lav mic, name tags on screen (overlay), pinned comment = ticket link, moderator script for common questions (price, schedule, wifi).
- **Post-LIVE:** clip the best 30-sec moment into a normal post within 2 hours. LIVE replays underperform raw clips.

### 4.3 On-the-day content rules

1. **Speed beats polish.** Post raw, authentic clips within 2 hours of the moment. TikTok punishes late content more than imperfect content.
2. **No copyrighted music issues:** use TikTok Commercial Music Library (event is commercial use).
3. **Consent first:** always ask before filming attendees; keep a "no photo" badge option for speakers.
4. **CTA on every post:** ticket link pinned in comments on ALL pre-event posts; "follow for day 2" on all during-event posts.
5. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Comments = algorithm fuel. Save 20 min/day purely for replying.

## 6. Hashtag & Search Strategy (TikTok)

- **Branded:** `#TechCon2027` (all posts)
- **Reach (3–4 per post):** `#techconference`, `#conferencevlog`, `#techcareers`, `#philippinestech`, `#startuplife`
- **Niche (2–3 per post):** topic-specific, e.g., `#aiforbusiness`, `#productivity`
- **Search optimization:** put the key phrase in the first 2 lines of the caption and SAY it in the first 3 seconds of the video — TikTok search picks up both.
- **Do not:** repeat the same 15 generic hashtags on every post. Rotate.

---

## 7. Engagement & Community Playbook

- **Duet/Stitch opportunities:** day before the event, duet the top 3 "what to expect at a tech conference" videos from creators.
- **Creators in the room:** list 10 local tech creators to invite with a free ticket in exchange for 1 post + 1 LIVE mention.
- **UGC fuel:** add a photo-booth wall with the branded hashtag; post a "Best of the photo wall" on day 2.
- **Crisis response (pre-written templates):** overcrowding complaint, speaker cancellation, ticket issue — 3 stock replies that stay calm, apologize, and move to DM/help desk. Social team's job is triage, not solving (hand off to ops).
