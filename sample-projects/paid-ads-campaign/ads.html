🎨 The ads people will see

4 Creative Concepts

Ad A · Static
"Weekdays 2–5 PM: 2nd drink 50% off"
Drink close-up + offer overlay. Warm color grade, light text, price visible in the first scroll.
Hook: "The shot people drive from Clark for"
Ad B · Reel 9s
Barista pulls the espresso
Raw, handheld, 9 seconds. Caption question to seed comments: "who's this for?"
Hook: "POV: your daily order, done right"
Ad C · Reel (retargeting)
"You watched this once."
Second-chance reel for people who saw Ad B but didn't visit. Direct: "Come see the pour."
Hook: "We noticed you liked the first sip"
Ad D · Carousel
Menu → combo → map
3 frames: menu, combo price, directions. Best for the promo audience (P3).
Hook: "Your merienda was never this cheap"
🎬 Start with 4 creatives max at ₱60–90/day each. After 2 weeks, kill the 2 lowest performers — most cost is wasted on creatives, not audiences.
🧪 Prove, don't guess

A/B Testing Plan

TestVariant AVariant BWindow
1 · HookQuestion: "Craving an iced ube latte?"Offer: "50% off your 2nd drink, 2–5 PM only"Weeks 2–3
2 · FormatStatic photo + overlay9-sec ReelWeeks 2–3
3 · CTA"Order ahead""Get directions"Weeks 3–4
  1. One variable at a time. Never test a headline while also changing the image.
  2. Minimum sample: ~₱60/day per variant for at least 10–20 days — early signals are not final truth.
  3. Winner + 48h rule: scale winners +20% every 2 days until CPC rises >10% or CTR drops >15%.
📡 Measure everything

Tracking & Measurement Setup

  • Meta Pixel installed on the website — even a simple order/DM page counts
  • UTM links: ?utm_source=meta_ad&utm_campaign=wk_v1&utm_content=creative_a
  • In-store promo code: ARAW30 — printed on the ad and a counter card, so redemptions are measurable
  • QR at the counter — same code, so offline visits are trackable too
  • Weekly dashboard in Google Sheets: spend, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, redemptions, cost per redemption
📅 Monday ritual15-min one-page report: what spent, what worked, what's cut, next test
💡 WhyClients pay for control and accountability — reporting is the product
