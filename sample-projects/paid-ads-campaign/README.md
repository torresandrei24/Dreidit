# Paid Ads Mini-Campaign — Araw Kape (Meta Ads)

**Prepared by:** Andrei Torres
**Role applied for:** Social Media Manager
**Portfolio sample project — Paid Ads Mini-Campaign**

> A 4-week Meta (Facebook/Instagram) paid ads plan for the same fictional
> client as my competitor audit — Araw Kape coffee shop, Angeles City.
> Budget, audience, creatives, A/B testing, tracking, and reporting —
> built to show I can spend a client's money responsibly and measure it.

---

## 1. Project Overview

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Client (fictional) | **Araw Kape** — small coffee shop, Angeles City, Pampanga |
| Campaign objective | Foot traffic + in-store redemptions (not vanity reach) |
| Platforms | Meta (Facebook + Instagram) with TikTok as test bed |
| Budget | **₱15,000/month** (small-shop level, zero-waste spend) |
| Duration | 4 weeks: 1 week setup, 2 weeks test, 1 week scale winners |
| KPI anchor | Cost per redemption ≤ ₱250 and 60+ redemptions |

### Why this campaign exists

Organic content (my 30-day calendar + strategy) builds the audience; paid
speeds it up. This plan is designed to *complement* the organic plan, not
replace it — paid amplifies the same hooks and offers.

---

## 2. Budget Breakdown (₱15,000/month)

| Bucket | Share | Amount | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prospecting (Reels/Reach) | 50% | ₱7,500 | Find new people near Angeles/Clark |
| Retargeting (visitors/followers) | 30% | ₱4,500 | Convert profiles we already warmed |
| Promo (offer campaign) | 20% | ₱3,000 | Weekday 2nd-drink 50% off — redemption driver |

**Rule we commit to:** every bucket has ONE job. No "post boosting" — boosting
likes is burning money. Everything must drive a visitation signal:
profile visits, location link clicks, or promo redemptions.

**Daily pacing (prospecting example):** ₱7,500 ÷ 28 days ≈ ₱270/day, split
into 2–3 ad sets at ₱90–135 each — enough data to read within a week.

---

## 3. Audience & Targeting

| Ad set | Audience | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 Prospecting** | 18–45 · radius 15 km around Angeles City (incl. Clark, Mabalacat) | IG Reels + FB Feed, Stories | Local reach is the goal; broad is fine with good creative |
| **P2 Retargeting** | IG/FB followers · 50%+ video viewers · website visitors (30 days) | All placements | Warm people convert 3–5× more — spend here, not on cold reach |
| **P3 Promo** | Engagers + others who opened DMs/ordered before | FB Feed + IG Stories | The offer needs a warm home to get redemptions |

**Interest hints (prospecting):** coffee shops, specialty coffee, freelancing,
co-working spaces, students (City College of Angeles). Keep max 3 interests —
narrow targeting raises costs and kills delivery fast.

## 4. Creative Concepts (4 to start)

| # | Format | Concept | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Static image (ad) | Drink close-up + offer overlay: "Weekdays 2–5 PM: 2nd drink 50% off" | "The 26-second shot people drive from Clark for" |
| B | Reel (prospecting) | Barista pulls the espresso, 9 sec, raw + caption question | "POV: your daily order, done right" |
| C | Reel/Story (retargeting) | "You watched this once. Come see the pour." | "We noticed you liked the first sip" |
| D | Carousel (promo) | 3 frames: menu → combo price → map/directions | "Your merienda was never this cheap" |

**Creative budget rule:** only test formats behind fixed hooks; for the first
two weeks, run 4 creatives max at ~₱60–90/day each, then kill the 2 lowest.

---

## 5. A/B Testing Plan

| Test | Variant A | Variant B | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (2 weeks) | Question: "Craving an iced ube latte?" | Offer: "50% off your 2nd drink, 2–5 PM only" | Aim for 10–20k impressions per variant before judging |
| Format (2 weeks) | Photo + overlay | Reel 9s | Reels usually win; verify with real data |
| CTA (2 weeks) | "Order ahead" | "Get directions" | Redemptions vs map clicks — decide while mid-flight |

**Rules we commit to:**

- One variable at a time — never Test a headline while changing the image.
- Min ~₱60/day per variant for at least 10–20 days (small budgets only give
  early signals, not final truth).
- Winner for 48h rule: keep the winner and scale 20% every 2 days until CPC
  rises >10% or CTR drops >15%.

---

## 6. Tracking & Measurement Setup

| Signal | How |
|---|---|
| Meta Pixel | Install on website (even a simple order/DM page) |
| UTMs | `?utm_source=meta_ad&utm_campaign=wk_v1&utm_content=creative_a` |
| In-store promo code | "ARAW30" — print on the ad (+ on a counter card) so redemption = measurable |
| QR at counter | Same promo code so offline visits are trackable |
| Weekly dashboard | Google Sheets: spend, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, redemptions, cost/redemption |

**Weekly ritual:** 15-min report to the client every Monday — one page: what
spent, what worked, what's cut, next test. Clients pay for time earned
control.

---

## 7. KPIs & Targets (4 weeks)

| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| CTR (link/reel) | 1.2%+ | Is the creative interesting enough? |
| CPC (link clicks) | ≤₱3 | Keep cost per visitor in check |
| CPM | ₱50–80 | Sanity check vs local benchmark |
| Promo redemptions | 60+ | The only number the owner feels |
| Cost per redemption | ≤₱250 | Is the ad cheaper than a coupon? |
| Foot traffic (weekdays) | +10% | Social ↔ store connection |

**Report template (send at day 28):** spend vs plan, best ad set, best
creative, redemptions, foot traffic delta, and "next month we'd change X".
Real ad account view: screenshot the Ads Manager result row and attach it.
