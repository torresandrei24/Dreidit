01 Logo System

OFF THE WALL
[ MANILA · MAKATI · Q.C. ]

Three marks: V-mark for avatars, stencil wordmark for banners, checker badge for stickers. One "V" clear space, no stretching, red on white or white on black only.

02 Color

Off Black#111111
Bone#FFFFFF
Checker Red#E30612
Concrete#2E2E2E

60% bone · 25% off black · 10% concrete · 5% red. Red is a weapon — one hero element per layout, never the whole frame.

03 Design Rules

DO: raw clips, deadpan captions, Taglish, checker accents, local spots, one bold move per layout.

DON'T: stock model shots, soft pastels, "uwu" captions, clutter. If a post could belong to any brand, it doesn't belong here.
