## 2. Brand Essence

- **Meaning:** "Off The Wall" = defying expectations; in PH it means skate
  culture living in barangay courts, parking lots, and DIY ramps.
- **Voice:** bold, deadpan-humor, Taglish on purpose; never corporate, never
  pastel; red is a weapon used once per layout.
- **Ratios:** 60% bone · 25% off black · 10% concrete · 5% checkered red.

## 3. Logo System (3 Concepts)

1. **V-Mark** — red rounded square, white V stroke. For social avatars and
   app icons.
2. **Stencil Wordmark** — Anton type, "OFF THE WALL PH" with city line
   [ MANILA · MAKATI · Q.C. ]. For banners and posts.
3. **Checker Badge** — class Vans checkerboard circle + red V. For stickers
   and Story frames.

Usage rules: clear space of one "V", min size, red-on-white/white-on-black/
all-black only, no stretching, no effects.

## 4. Color & Typography

- **Palette:** Off Black #111111 · Bone #FFFFFF · Checkered Red #E20000 ·
  Concrete #2E2E2E · Asphalt #F4F2EE
- **Display:** Anton (condensed, loud — headlines, covers, events)
- **Body:** Quicksand (round, friendly — captions, copy)

## 5. Social Templates

- **Promo post (1:1):** offer first, logo bottom-left, code `WALL20`
- **Quote card:** statement type on red, one idea, one word mark
- **Story frame (9:16):** halftone + sticker badge + UGC prompt

## 6. Campaign Concept — "Skate Pinas"

Monthly open skate contest in a different city spot (parking lots, barangay
courts, DIY ramps — no rented venues). Free entry; best trick wins shoes +
board; all entries post with #SkatePinas. Sequence: spot reveal → skater
spotlights → live coverage → best-trick clips → next-city teaser. One event
produces a week of content; UGC runs the engine.
